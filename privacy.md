# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The Data Read pipeline is a private tool used only by The Data Read to manage videos on its own YouTube channel ([@TheDataRead](https://www.youtube.com/@TheDataRead)).

**What it accesses.** Through Google's YouTube Data API, it updates the title, description, tags, captions and settings of videos on The Data Read's own channel, and posts comments on those videos. It does not access any other person's account or data.

**What it collects.** Nothing about viewers. No personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared.

**YouTube and Google.** Use of YouTube is governed by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Revoking access.** The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
